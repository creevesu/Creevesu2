How to play
  • House people first: huts give islanders beds. Islanders work your market and keep the island alive.
  • Power everything: solar panels and wind turbines feed huts, hotels, bars and markets. Run a deficit and happiness (and income) tanks.
  • Tourists pay the bills: hotels house them, beach bars and palms make them happy, a lighthouse puts your island on the map.
  • Docks need water: place them on sand touching the sea for daily fishing income.
  • Watch the weather: storms wreck a building now and then. Tap the rubble to repair it, or bulldoze it clear.
  • Every day ticks by on its own — income, upkeep, arrivals and departures all settle at sunrise.
